Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

SariMe is an offline-first Point of Sale (POS) and Customer Credit (Utang) Ledger application designed for micro-retail and sari-sari store owners.

1. Local-First Data Ownership & Privacy (RA 10173 / GDPR Compliance)

  • 100% On-Device Store Database: All products, inventory counts, sales transactions, profit reports, and customer credit (utang) records are stored locally in an SQLite database inside your device's private application storage.
  • No Personal Data Harvesting: SariMe does not collect, upload, sell, or transmit your store inventory, financial records, or customer lists to any external cloud server.
  • User Control & Data Deletion: You retain full ownership of your data. You can export a local JSON backup or permanently erase all data at any time via Settings > Reset All Store Data or by uninstalling the application.

2. Device Permissions Disclosure

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely in real time to scan product barcodes and attach optional local product images. Camera streams are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used strictly to check GitHub Releases and GitHub Pages for application version updates, service announcements, and maintenance status. No store or customer data is transmitted during update checks.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used for on-device alerts such as low-stock warnings and customer credit due-date reminders.

3. Terms of Use & Accounting Disclaimer

  • As-Is Software Provision: SariMe is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind. Store owners are responsible for verifying their own physical cash counts, credit records, and regular local backup files.
  • Non-Fiscal Bookkeeping Aid: SariMe is a digital logbook replacement to assist small storekeepers with internal tracking and does not replace official Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) registered fiscal point-of-sale machines where required by law.
